Prefer to ask a person? Email questions@lassmanstudios.com — we answer every graduate and every parent.

How do I book my graduation session?

Start with the school code provided by your school or studio. The booking service will show the sessions available for your school.

What should I wear?

Choose something you feel comfortable and confident in. Check your school's session instructions for specific clothing, gown, and accessory guidance.

Can I change an existing appointment?

Yes. Choose "Find or change your appointment" to access the appointment management flow. Check your confirmation email for the details you need.

Where can I view and order my photos?

Choose "View my photos" to go to the customer photo gallery. Have the access details supplied by the studio ready.
